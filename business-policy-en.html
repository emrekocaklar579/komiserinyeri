Business Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

This Business Policy applies to all orders placed at Komiserin Yeri, including dine-in, takeaway, online QR orders, and delivery via Grab. By placing an order, you agree to the terms below.

1. Shipping & Delivery

Dine-in & takeaway: Orders placed at our café are prepared fresh and served or handed to you at the counter. Typical preparation time is 10–25 minutes depending on order size and current demand.

Online orders (QR menu): Orders placed through our online menu are processed during our opening hours. You will be notified when your order is ready for pickup at the store.

Grab delivery: Delivery is handled by Grab and subject to Grab’s own terms, fees, and estimated delivery times. Delivery times may vary due to traffic, weather, or high demand. Komiserin Yeri is not responsible for delays caused by the delivery partner once the order has been handed over for delivery.

Non-delivery or late delivery: If you do not receive your order or experience a significant delay, please contact us immediately at +66 064 094 7020 or admin@komiserinyeri.com. For Grab orders, you may also report the issue through the Grab app.

2. Cancellation Policy

Before preparation: You may cancel your order free of charge if it has not yet entered preparation. Please contact us as soon as possible by phone or email, or notify our staff at the counter.

After preparation has started: Once food or beverages have been prepared, orders generally cannot be cancelled because they are made fresh to order.

Grab orders: Cancellation of Grab delivery orders must be requested before the order is confirmed for preparation. After confirmation, cancellation is subject to Grab’s platform rules and our kitchen status.

Pre-orders or special requests: For large orders or special catering requests, cancellation must be made at least 24 hours in advance. Late cancellations may incur a charge for ingredients already purchased or prepared.

3. Returns, Refunds & Replacements

Because we sell fresh food and beverages, items that have been prepared, served, or delivered are generally not eligible for return.

Quality issues: If you receive an incorrect item, a damaged product, or food that does not meet our quality standards, please contact us within 24 hours with your order details and, if possible, a photo. We will review your case and may offer a replacement, store credit, or refund at our discretion.

Refund method: Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method where possible. Processing may take 5–14 business days depending on your bank or payment provider.

Non-refundable cases: We reserve the right to decline refunds for orders consumed in full, orders cancelled after preparation, or cases where no valid proof of issue is provided.

Grab orders: Refund requests for delivery orders may also be processed through the Grab app in accordance with Grab’s refund procedures.
